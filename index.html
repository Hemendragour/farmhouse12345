About The Project

More Than Land.
A Lifestyle.

The Lodhi Farms Phase 2 offers spacious farmhouse plots for those seeking a private retreat, a weekend farmhouse, or a peaceful green lifestyle within reach of Bhopal. Set along Kolar Road in Amrabad / Amravat Kalan, this phase is designed for people who want room to breathe — without leaving the city too far behind.

Plot sizes from 5,000 to 10,000 sq.ft. — room to design the farmhouse you envision
Located on Kolar Road, Amrabad / Amravat Kalan, Bhopal
Core infrastructure in place — wide roads, electricity, water supply and security
